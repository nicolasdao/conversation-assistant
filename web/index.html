Speaker mode

The call is playing through the Mac's speakers, so the microphone hears the guests too. Their words would be transcribed a second time, as if you had said them.

So the microphone is muted while the call plays, and for a quarter of a second after. Anything said over a guest in that time is not transcribed or recorded.

For the best transcript, use earbuds or headphones. Speaker mode turns off by itself as soon as they are connected.
