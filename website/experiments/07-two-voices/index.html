System 1

Two voices, one transcript. Your microphone and the call your Mac plays, transcribed as they speak. Each voice becomes a speaker you can name. Works with any call app.

Every word lands on a timeline. Topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations and clip-worthy moments.

Claims checked as they're said. System 1 flags a checkable claim in about 0.4 s. System 2 researches the web and puts a sourced verdict on screen within seconds.
