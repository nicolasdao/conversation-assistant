Tattle

It transcribes live conversations, your microphone and the call your Mac plays, maps them on a timeline, and fact-checks claims as they're said.

Three platforms, one conversation

1

Transcribe

The transcript streams as people speak. Each voice is recognised as a speaker, and you can name them. It works with any call app, because it listens to the Mac's own audio.

2

Map

A timeline lane for each: topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations, and clip-worthy moments.

3

Fact-check

System 1 (Jev, a fast decision model) flags checkable claims in about 0.4 s each, some 2,000 judgments an hour for cents. System 2, a slower model that researches the web, puts a sourced verdict on screen within seconds, and makes System 1 better.
