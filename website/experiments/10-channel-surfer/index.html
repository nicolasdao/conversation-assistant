TV guide

Tonight on your Mac

  1. 20:00CH 1

    Live transcript

    Your microphone and the call your Mac plays, written down as people speak. Each voice is recognised as a speaker, and you can name them. Works with any call app.

  2. 20:30CH 2

    Timeline

    The show mapped as it runs: topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations, and clip-worthy moments.

  3. 21:00CH 3

    Fact-check

    Sourced verdicts on screen within seconds: Supported, Contradicted, Misleading, or Unverifiable. A fast model flags checkable claims in about 0.4 s; a slower one researches the web and checks them.

  4. 21:30CH 4

    Your Mac only

    No server, no account, no analytics. Recordings and keys stay on your Mac.

  5. LateRepeats

    After the show

    Chat about the transcript with ⌘K, live or afterwards. Every show lands in a recordings library: play it back at up to 4×, or export it as one file to send.
