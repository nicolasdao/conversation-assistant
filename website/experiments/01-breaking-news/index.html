Seg 01

The transcript · the timelineEvery word, as it's said

It listens to your microphone and the call your Mac plays, so it works with any call app.

Built for a live podcast: the host's microphone plus the guests on the call. The transcript streams as people speak. Each voice is recognised as a speaker, whom you can name.

Then it maps the show on a timeline: topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations, and clip-worthy moments.
