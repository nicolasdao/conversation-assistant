What happens on your Mac

Built for a live podcast: the host's microphone plus the guests on the call. It works with any call app, because it listens to the Mac's own audio.

01The transcript streams

As people speak. Each voice is recognised as a speaker, whom you can name.

HostOkay, enough about models, how was surfing in Sydney this weekend?
GuestI don't buy that at all, cheap is not the same as good.
HostLightning never strikes the same place twice.

02It maps on a timeline

Topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations, and clip-worthy moments.

03Claims get checked

System 1 (Jev, a fast decision model, about 0.4 s a judgment) flags checkable claims. System 2, a slower LLM that researches the web, checks them. Sourced verdicts land on screen within seconds.

Before you press the button

Keys
It needs two API keys, from OpenAI and OpenRouter, with prepaid credit. The app walks you through both.
Cost
About $1.60 per hour of show, or about $1.23 for a transcript only.
Privacy
No server, no account, no analytics. Recordings and keys stay on your Mac.
Afterwards
Chat about the transcript (⌘K). Play recordings back at up to 4×, and export one as a single file to send.
Trust
Signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself. BSD 3-Clause.
Needs
Apple Silicon, macOS 14.2 or later.
