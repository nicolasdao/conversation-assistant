Cold open Episode 01 · 60 min

It listens. It maps. It checks the facts.

Tattle is an open-source Mac app that transcribes live conversations, maps them on a timeline, and fact-checks claims as they're said.

Segment 1 What it is

A live transcript, mapped while you talk.

  • Your mic and the call your Mac plays. Any call app, because it listens to the Mac's own audio.
  • Every voice becomes a speaker you can name.
  • One timeline: topic, mode, heat and hype, hot takes, predictions, disagreements, clip-worthy moments.
  • Chat with the transcript (⌘K), live or afterwards.

Segment 2 Fact-checks

Claims checked while they're still being said.

System 1 flags checkable claims in about 0.4 s each. System 2 researches the web, checks them, and makes System 1 better.

Segment 3 Your Mac, your data

No server. No account. No analytics.

  • Recordings and keys stay on your Mac.
  • Two API keys, from OpenAI and OpenRouter, with prepaid credit. The app walks you through both.
  • About $1.60 per hour of show, or about $1.23 for a transcript only.
  • Signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself. BSD 3-Clause.

Outro That's the show

Your next conversation, on the timeline.
