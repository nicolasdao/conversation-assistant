Listens to
Your microphone and the call your Mac plays, in any call app. Ask the transcript anything with ⌘K, live or afterwards.
Keeps
Recordings and keys stay on your Mac. A recordings library, playback at up to 4×, and one-file exports to send.
Costs
Two API keys, from OpenAI and OpenRouter, with prepaid credit; the app walks you through both. About $1.60 per hour of show, or $1.23 for a transcript only.
