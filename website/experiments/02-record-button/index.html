Rundown
What happens after you press record

Tonight's show

  1. 01On air

    Listens

    Your microphone and the call your Mac plays, as two voices. It works with any call app, because it listens to the Mac's own audio.

    Host · mic
    Guest · call
  2. 02On air

    Transcribes

    The transcript streams as people speak. Each voice is recognised as a speaker, whom you can name.

    HostOkay, enough about models, how was surfing in Sydney this weekend?

    Speaker 2 I don't buy that at all, cheap is not the same as good.

  3. 03On air

    Maps

    The conversation lands on a timeline: topic, mode, heat and hype, disagreements, hot takes, predictions, recommendations and clip-worthy moments.

    Topic
    AI modelsPersonal lifeTech
    Mode
    NewsAnalysisBanterExplainer
    Heat
    Hype
    Pins
    Hot takePredictionClip
  4. 04On air

    Fact-checks

    Sourced verdicts on screen within seconds. System 1, Jev, a fast decision model at about 0.4 s a judgment, flags the checkable claims. System 2, a slower LLM that researches the web, checks them and makes System 1 better.

    Supported

    "Octopuses have three hearts."

    Misleading

    "Coffee dehydrates you."

    Its water outweighs the mild diuretic effect.

  5. 05On air

    After the show

    Chat about the transcript, live or afterwards. Every show goes into a recordings library: play it back at up to 4×, or export it as one file to send.

    ⌘K4×Export

0 servers

No server, no account, no analytics. Recordings and keys stay on your Mac.

Signed

Signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself. Open source under BSD 3-Clause.
