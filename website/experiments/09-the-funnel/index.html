Before you press the button

Any call app
It listens to your microphone and to your Mac's own audio, so it works with whatever you call on.
Two keys
It needs API keys from OpenAI and OpenRouter, with prepaid credit. The app walks you through both.
Running cost
About $1.60 per hour of show, or about $1.23 for a transcript only.
Private
No server, no account, no analytics. Recordings and keys stay on your Mac.
Trusted
Signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself. BSD 3-Clause.
